Where Rainbows Meet

Transforming Lives • Building Communities • Creating Hope

A registered non-profit organization based in Vrygrond, Cape Town, serving as a holistic community development center offering access to education, health support, food security, and empowerment opportunities.

Our Mission: Uplift and empower vulnerable individuals and families by addressing the root causes of poverty, crime, and unemployment through integrated programs and outreach services.

The Challenges We Face

Vrygrond - the oldest informal settlement in the Western Cape - faces significant challenges. But where others see problems, we see opportunities for transformation.

Our Strategic Focus

👥

Comprehensive Development

Programs for children, youth, women, and the unemployed across all life stages.

🎯

Skills & Employment

Work readiness training, business support, income generation, and driver's license programs for disadvantaged youth.

🤝

Community Services

Expanding awareness, legal, and social services through ECD, aftercare, and youth development.

🍽️

Food Security

Ensuring food security with 7+ million meals annually through our nutritional feeding program.

✊

Empowerment

Promoting dignity, security, and self-reliance through awareness and psychosocial support.
